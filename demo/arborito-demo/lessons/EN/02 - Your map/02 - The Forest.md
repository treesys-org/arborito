@section
index: 1
title: Courses
@/section

In the nav, **Courses** opens the panel where you keep what you open, import, or create.

There are two tabs:

- **My courses:** yours on this device (and anything synced with your account).
- **Explore:** public courses from the network.

@section
index: 2
title: Create or import
@/section

At the bottom, **Create** lets you choose:

- **Course:** a new full course in your garden.
- **Playlist:** several courses in one path.

You can also **Import file** (a shared `.arborito`).

@section
index: 3
title: Playlist
@/section

A **playlist** is not a single course: it joins **several courses** into one path.

Use it when you want to study or share **more than one course together** (for example language + grammar).

From **Create → Playlist**, or by opening **Playlists** in Courses, you can build, open, or import one.

@/section

Some courses (like this demo) are not edited in place. In **construction mode** you can create a named copy and edit yours.
