@section
index: 1
title: What is Arborito
@/section

Arborito is a **study app with a map**: open folders, read lessons, practise with questions. You can also play games with those same questions.

@image
url:./media/02-mapa-claro-en.png
caption: The map: how a course looks.
@/image

@section
index: 2
title: Folders and lessons
@/section

- A **folder** is a topic with lessons inside.
- A **leaf** is a lesson.
- Tap the leaf to open the lesson.

@quiz
concept: Map and lessons
items:
  - question: What opens when you tap a leaf on the map?
    answer: A lesson
    modes: multiple,recall
    traps:
      - Only Arcade
      - The whole Courses panel
      - A trophy
  - question: What is a folder on the map?
    answer: A topic with lessons inside
    modes: multiple,recall
    traps:
      - A locked exam
      - Only a game
      - The trash
@/quiz

@section
index: 3
title: Your progress
@/section

Progress stays **on this device**. The `.arborito` file is the **course**, not your progress.

@quiz

  - question: What file do you share to give someone a course?

    modes: multiple,recall
