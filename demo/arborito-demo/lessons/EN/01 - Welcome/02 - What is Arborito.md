@section
index: 1
title: What is Arborito
@/section

Arborito is a **study app with a map**: open folders, read lessons, practise with questions. You can also play games with those same questions.

@image
url:./media/02-mapa-claro-en.png
caption: The map: how a course looks.
@/image

@section
index: 2
title: Folders and lessons
@/section

- A **folder** is a topic.
- A **leaf** is a lesson.
- Tap the leaf to open the text.

@quiz
concept: Map and branches
items:
  - question: What opens when you tap a leaf?
    answer: A lesson
    modes: multiple,recall
    traps:
      - Only Arcade
      - The whole Forest
      - A trophy
  - question: What is a folder on the map?
    answer: A topic with lessons inside
    modes: multiple,recall
    traps:
      - A locked exam
      - Only a game
      - The trash
@/quiz

@section
index: 3
title: Your progress
@/section

Progress stays **on this device**. A `.arborito` file carries the course **content**, not your history.

@quiz
concept: Forest and data
items:
  - question: Where is progress saved by default?
    answer: On this device
    modes: multiple,recall
    traps:
      - In the .arborito file
      - Only online
      - In Classroom
  - question: What file do you share for a course?
    answer: .arborito
    modes: multiple,recall
    traps:
      - PDF only
      - .mp4
      - An Arcade link
@/quiz
