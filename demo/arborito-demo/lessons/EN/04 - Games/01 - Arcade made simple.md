@section
index: 1
title: What Arcade is
@/section

**Arcade** is mini-games **inside** Arborito. They use your lesson questions: study once, play many times.

@image
url:./media/07-arcade-en.png
caption: Pick a game with the course open.
@/image

@section
index: 2
title: How to enter
@/section

1. Open a folder on the map.
2. Tap **Play** 🎮.
3. Pick Classroom, Memory, Hacky Terminal, Alonso Duel…

**Static:** no AI. **Dynamic:** with Sage (if you turned him on).
